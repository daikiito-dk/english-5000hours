## Logs

### Example: The Power of Tiny Gains - 2026-09-21
- **媒体 / ソース**: James Clear - Atomic Habits
- **要約 (Summary)**: Improving by just 1% every day results in a 37-fold improvement over the course of a year. Habits are the compound interest of self-improvement.
- **重要フレーズ・表現**:
  - *compound interest* (複利)
  - *marginal gains* (わずかな改善の積み重ね)
- **自分の考え (Reflection)**: 5,000時間のプロジェクトも、1日3.3時間の積み重ね。焦らず毎日の習慣を固定化することが最重要。
